## Examples of use that requires a commercial license

The following are **not covered** by the free license, regardless of who is doing them — an individual, a freelancer, a startup, or an established company:

- Using Zaelix AI (as-is or modified) inside a paid client project or client deliverable

- Selling, sublicensing, or white-labeling Zaelix AI or a fork of it
- Using Zaelix AI to run internal business operations for a company or startup

- Deploying Zaelix AI on behalf of clients as part of a paid consulting, agency, or freelance engagement

- Academic research, coursework, or teaching use
